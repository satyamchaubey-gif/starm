Procurement Framework Templates

Standard operating templates aligned with DPIIT and MSInS innovative procurement guidelines.

1. Outcome-Based Problem Statement Template

  • Title: Objective-focused heading (avoiding brand-specific requirements).
  • Current Baseline: Quantitative operational failure rate or friction metric.
  • Target Outcome: Clear target KPI (e.g., >30% efficiency boost in 90 days).
  • Sandbox Environment: Department pilot site details, access limits, test cohorts.

2. Exemption & Evaluation Framework

  • Relaxation Criteria: Complete waiver on prior turnover and prior experience clauses (under Rule 173(i) GFR 2017).
  • Screening (30%): Technical feasibility and DPIIT startup validation.
  • Pilot Scoring (50%): Sandbox KPI benchmark verification over 60–90 days.
  • Scalability (20%): Cost-per-unit predictability and state-wide rollout readiness.

3. Data Governance & Intellectual Property (IP) Clauses

  • Background IP: Complete ownership retained by the startup for pre-existing code and algorithms.
  • Department Data: All state citizen data remains sovereign property of the Government of Maharashtra; cannot be extracted or trained externally.
  • Commercial Exploitation: Startup retains global rights to product; department receives perpetual, royalty-free operational license.

4. Direct Scale-up & Milestone Contracting

  • Milestone 1 (30% payment): Sandbox integration and baseline metric logging.
  • Milestone 2 (40% payment): Mid-term validation report by designated independent expert.
  • Milestone 3 (30% payment): Final evaluation and deployment clearance.
  • Direct Scale-up: Successful pilots trigger direct institutional procurement without re-tendering under proprietary innovation provisions.
